Artist · Developer · Montreal

Hugo Waterfall

Hugo Waterfall is a Canadian-American digital artist and creative technologist based in Montreal, studying Computer Science and Computation Arts at Concordia University.

Specializing in 2D digital art across horror and dark fantasy, Hugo focuses on narrative-driven character design, particularly visual metamorphosis that mirrors psychological and narrative transformation. His background blends visual storytelling with technical implementation, leveraging tools like Blender, Ren’Py, and Python to turn static concept art into interactive worlds.

A veteran of custom character commissions with a longstanding background in video editing and audio-visual storytelling, Hugo is currently developing Womb of the Gods, a narrative-driven choose-your-own-adventure game.
