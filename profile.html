Hugo Waterfall


Hugo Waterfall is a Canadian-American artist based in Montreal. He is currently enrolled in the Computer Science and Computation Arts Joint Major program at Concordia University.

Hugo specializes in digital 2D art, frequently working in the horror and fantasy genres. He is passionate about character design and the role visual transformation can play in storytelling, using changes in a character’s appearance to reflect their experiences and the progression of the narrative.

He is also learning software engineering and web application skills to develop his creative projects. He is skilled in Java and Python and has experience working with Flask, implementing AI models, and coding in C.

His previous creative pursuits include co-founding an art commission business in high school, in which Hugo drafted and completed dozens of illustrations based on clients’ original characters. Additionally, he has been writing, filming, and editing short YouTube videos as a hobby since he was young, weaving together his love of music and cinema. Recently, he has begun working in Blender to build static 3-D models and create short animations.

Hugo is currently writing and developing a choose-your-own-adventure game called Womb of the Gods using Ren’Py.
