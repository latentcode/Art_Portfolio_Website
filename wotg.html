Womb   of   the   Gods


Overview

Womb of the Gods is a choose-your-own-adventure role-playing game that is being developed using Ren'Py. Womb of the Gods is still in the early stages of development; however, a playable prototype is available below.



Narrative Premise

The story follows Mouse, a bounty hunter living in the isolated police-state of Bol Santo, a place where interaction with the outside world is strictly forbidden. After losing his husband, Mouse carries out a plan to venture beyond the Bol Santo borders. His escape into unfamiliar terrain soon lands him in the middle of a cultist ritual, where he encounters an Eldritch species called Glum - ethereal beings who have determined that the human body is the perfect biological vessel to birth their progeny. Forced to engage in this ritual, Mouse Ascends - a process that plants a nascent Glum deep within Mouse’s mortal flesh, who will continue to grow until it is ready to claw its way out. Marked for death, Mouse embarks on a desperate quest for survival, attempting to harness the otherworldy power brewing within him to repel the Glum and eradicate the cult that worships them. Pitted against monsters, mobsters, cultists, and dictators - as well as a bloodthirsty psychopath, a savage vampire, and the relentless Boogeyman - Mouse must navigate foreign lands, find allies, and ultimately perservere lest the Glum consume him and everything he holds dear.



Womb of the Gods (Game Prototype) 🖱️

November 2023 · Visual Studio Code / Photoshop


This early prototype for Womb of the Gods, made with just html, css, and javascript, is a small choose-your-own-adventure game I made for class to study the basics of web development. I planned, structured, and wrote a branching narrative around some of my existing art, created original artworks to suit certain narrative plot points, then included instrumental music from some of my favorite media to accompany the story. The goal of the game is to experience all nine endings.
