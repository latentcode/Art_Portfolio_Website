Vessels of Ascension

April, June 2026 · Photoshop · Womb of the Gods


A two-part project made for Womb of the Gods that showcases two of the story's key characters. The first portrait depicts Nezmahil Ikram, a recurring antagonist, whose body is puppeted by unknowable cosmic forces. The second depicts Sylvie Talax, a potential ally, who must weigh her pursuit of vengeance against her desperate mission to purge the eyeballs nestled in her skin.



Polygonal Activity

March 2026 · Blender / Adobe Premiere · CART 362


Set in a virtual recreation of our 3D Digital Production classroom, André Neder and I made this project to simulate reality within Blender, then leverage Blender's physics engine to highlight how the simulation differs from real life and takes on a reality of its own. We aimed to expose reality as a fragile construct that is ultimately overtaken by the logic of its own digital representation. Read the final report.



Womb of the Gods Character Lineup

February 2026 · Photoshop · Womb of the Gods


Concept art of the six most prominent characters in the Womb of the Gods, experimenting with facial expressions, outfits, and relative character sizes. The individuals shown, from left to right, are Jez Kilnan, Sylvie Talax, Nezmahil Ikram, Ra'hin Abdul, Estrella, and Fiona Delafor.



Successive Adaptation

February 2026 · Blender / Photoshop · CART 362


My 3-part Blender project tells the story of nature, as represented by a solitary dog, being engulfed by the tides of human industry, before emerging again, free but clearly tainted. Here, nature is depicted with respect to its own corruption, where the oil and the animal are defined by one another, intertwined in this process of biological succession. Read the final report.

Journal: Mood Analyzer 🖱️

December 2025 · Visual Studio Code · CART 351


An interactive website developed by André Neder, Owen Hill, and I that allows users to publicly publish journal entries, which are then processed by a high-tech artificial intelligence that determines each entry's sentimentality. These entries are then displayed graphically as a collective to show their sentimentally color ratings.

War

November 2025 · Blender / Adobe Premiere · CART 361


Meant to be played on a loop, this Blender project depicts the inevitability of war and the repetition of history. In accordance with the symbol of Ouroboros, these events reflect how society’s actions often end up undoing the progress it has made. In this animation, as we follow the character through the ages, humanity’s innate lust for conflict always drags the world back to the Stone Age, cementing its legacy as one big tragic cycle. Read the final report.

Flaskemy Feud! 🖱️

November 2025 · Visual Studio Code · CART 351


An interactive website developed by André Neder and I that allows users to interact with a simplified version of the gameshow Family Feud (1976). Users have the option to compete in the gameshow, in which they try to guess the most submitted answers to a series of questions, or submit answers to a survey that directly feeds into subsequent gameshows. Although the website has only a few questions, it is highly flexible and can be easily modified to cover any topic.

Website Final Report GitHub

Womb of the Gods (Game Prototype) 🖱️

November 2023 · Visual Studio Code / Photoshop · Womb of the Gods / CART 211


A choose-your-own-adventure role-playing game made to study the basics of web development. I planned, structured, and wrote a branching narrative around some of my existing art, created original artworks to suit certain narrative plot points, then included instrumental music from some of my favorite media to accompany the story. The goal of the game is to experience all nine endings.
