    Written in 2023, Margarette is a short story I drafted and completed throughout the creative writing course ENGL 224. Set in the 19th century, it chronicles the life of a man who becomes increasingly infatuated with his wife's eyes, a dangerous obsession that pushes him further and further off the deep end.
