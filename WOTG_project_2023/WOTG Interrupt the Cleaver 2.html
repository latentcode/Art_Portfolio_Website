The Last Stand

There she stands, the bloodied tiefling barely managing to stay upright in the corner. She shouts at the three of us as we approach.

"They will rise no matter what, whether you animals live or die. I want to make it clear: this is not for Them. This is for me," she growls, "and for my sister."

You watch as she punches the ground with all her might. The ground cracks, then begins to boil beneath you. A pool of flames erupts under your feet. The three of you attempt to roll out of the way, but the insectoid gets hit with the full heat of the attack. You and Fiona manage to dodge forward through the flames, in pursuit of the tiefling.

You use your Eldritch arm to sweep under the tiefling’s feet, knocking her to the floor. Once there, you and Fiona are able to pounce. Using the arm as a massive club, you are able to hit so hard that the floor dents under the weight of each attack. The arm pummels the tiefling into the ground until she finally takes action.

Unable to take the hits anymore, the woman slaps her hands together and a magical electric force shoves you and Fiona back through the erupting flames. Through the fire you see her slowly limping toward you. Lightning crackles all around her body. She looks as if she is channeling something from deep within her very soul. Enormous beams of electricity shoot out all around her, wreaking havoc on the hellish room you are stuck in. You duck the stray lightning bolts flying toward you, when you notice the walls and the ceiling start to crumble. If you do not act now, all of you will perish.

The only way to stop her is the arm, if you can only figure out how to fully control it. Desperate, you decide to stick your fingers into the stump of the arm, attempting to pull on the nerves. Somehow, you manage to flex its bicep. Determined to end this, you lift the arm into the air and flick your wrist, sending the arm careening toward the floor at incredible speeds. The tiefling attempts to jump out of the way, but the arm flies directly into her abdomen, crushing her against the floor. The impact creates a 10-foot deep crater in the earth and the Eldritch arm goes limp. Below the arm, you see her lightning crackle and arc, until it fizzles out for good.

“Desmond!” you hear Fiona cry out. “Oh God…” She whispers as she runs over to him. You help the insectoid to his feet, then walk over to join them. On the way, you notice the portal gradually closing, and whatever was being summoned now slinks back into the void.

Check in with your friends.
