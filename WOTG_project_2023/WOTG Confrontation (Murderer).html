Confrontation

Deep within the palace walls, you are hit with a putrid stench wafting through a crack in the doorway across from you. An intense glow radiates from inside the chamber, pulsing to the rhythm of a heartbeat. You feel your mind being pierced, allowing horrendous screams to seep in. You feel the suffering of a thousand souls in your heart, stopping you dead in your tracks. This is so much more exciting than anything you've experienced before. Alas, there is nothing left to do, no more preparations to make. It’s now or never. You steel your resolve and enter.

You gaze upon two humanoid figures standing in front of a window, engaged in some sort of ritual. You recognize one of them: it's the woman from the painting, though now horrendously disfigured. She is missing the top half of her head, and in its place a bony hand reaches out.

A portal, identical to the one you passed through earlier, rests on the ground. Through it, a writhing mass of fleshy body parts seems to be crawling its way through, squelching as it makes contact with the hardwood floor. A sense of clarity washes over you. These are the beasts you are meant to kill. You know what you must do.

Exterminate them.
