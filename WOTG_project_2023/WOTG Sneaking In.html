Sneaking In

You hunch down as low to the ground as you can get, carefully cock your gun’s hammer, and step into the chamber. The two women seem completely transfixed on the portal and on the mound of flesh struggling to crawl out.

As you find a dark corner to sneak into, you notice the fleshy amalgamation pulse and squirm like a heartbeat, radiating energy as it does so. The more you examine it, the more you realize how irregular the beat is, as if it intentionally speeds up and slows down depending on what it is thinking. The hand sticking through the disfigured woman’s head twitches in response to the pulsations, almost as if it's being fed information. This is the most you can make out before you aim your sights at one of the enemies before you.

Just before you squeeze the trigger, the hand protruding out of the woman suddenly opens. Through the woman’s head, the arm shoots up, bending and cracking unnaturally as it straightens out to its full length. The ten feet of horrifying skin and bones locks on to you and lunges at you, taking the woman with it. You just barely dodge out of the way when it crashes next to you, obliterating the floor you were just standing on. The disfigured woman whips around behind it, almost comically, hitting the floor and breaking her shoulder. You fire your remaining two bullets at her hoping that it would somehow stop the arm, but to no avail.

“Did you really think we didn’t know you were coming?!” the tiefling in royal attire shouts over at you. “They’ve had their eyes on you for hours.” She motions to the mound of flesh, now pulsing rapidly as if extremely distressed.

The arm lunges at you again, but you manage to duck beneath it as it flies over your head and collides with a wall twenty feet away. The woman attached to it takes the full brunt of the impact, looking critically injured but emotionally unfazed.

Without a weapon, you are seriously running out of options.

What last-ditch effort do you make?

1) Pretend like it was all a misunderstanding.
2) Accept your fate.
