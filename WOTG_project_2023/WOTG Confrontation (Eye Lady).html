Confrontation

Deep within the palace walls, you are hit with a putrid stench wafting through a crack in the doorway across from you. An intense glow radiates from inside the chamber, pulsing to the rhythm of a heartbeat. You feel your mind being pierced, allowing horrendous screams to seep in. You feel the suffering of a thousand souls in your heart, stopping you dead in your tracks. Alas, there is nothing left to do, no more preparations to make. It’s now or never. You steel your resolve and enter.

Nothing could have prepared you for the wretched, repugnant contents of the room that attack all your senses at once. The smell hits you first, rotten enough to make a dead man faint. A bloody glob of mashed up body parts falls from the ceiling into your eye. You frantically wipe it off as your vision focuses on the scene just before you.

Two humanoid figures stand at the far end in front of a window, engaged in some sort of ritual. You recognize one of them: it's the woman from the painting, though now horrendously disfigured. She is missing the top half of her head, and in its place a bony hand reaches out.

A portal, identical to the one you passed through earlier, rests on the ground. Through it, a writhing mass of fleshy body parts seems to be crawling its way through, squelching as it makes contact with the hardwood floor. A sense of clarity washes over you. These women are the only thing keeping the portals open; with their defeat, London can be spared from whatever horrors they want to unleash. However, the unknowable power they are conjuring from the depths could make you into a god. It’s time to make a choice.

What will you do?

1) Accompany the strange eyeball-ridden woman and face the evil.
2) Negotiate with the two women and try to seize some power for yourself.
