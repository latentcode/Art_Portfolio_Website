Friendly Introduction

“Hello!” You shout out to the hunched over figure, still digging in the trash for something. You hope that a friendly and direct opener will ease any potential tension between you and this woman. Instead, she has her hands around your throat before you can blink.

“Where is it?” She asks calmly and simply, seemingly unsurprised by your intrusion. “It was right here, where is it?”

The pressure around your windpipe eases just enough for you to speak. “Is it that thing? Right over there?” You reply unsure, gesturing at a glowing shard of glass on the ground, just visible under a piece of rebar. The light emanating from it pulses with the same rhythm as a heartbeat.

“Yes, yes!” She exclaims giddy with relief. She lets go of your neck and you take a moment to regain your breath. You watch as she slips the glass into her magical staff, causing the entire staff to glow with the same rhythm.

You take this opportunity to ask, “Do you know anything about a portal linking this world with London? Under my Queen’s orders, I must destroy it.”

“Destroy? HA! I can take you to the people responsible. With your help, we can destroy them.”

She throws a damp burlap sack across her shoulder and looks up at me expectantly. Before I can answer, she takes my arm and leads me out of the valley. “I won’t end up like her,” she mutters as we exit. In front of us stands a mountain with a palace atop it. Our destination.

You swear, within the time since you met her, even more eyeballs have sprouted on her skin. Being near her makes you deeply uncomfortable.

Continue forth with the stranger.
