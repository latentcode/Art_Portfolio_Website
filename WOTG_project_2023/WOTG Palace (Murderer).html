To the Center

You venture forth down a large hallway adorned with paintings and pictures of the same quality as the one you just encountered. The interior is beautiful, though unintentionally so. The pristine palace is now overgrown with vines and flowers showcasing every color in the rainbow. This is a remarkable contrast to the wasteland you just came out of.

The sweet aroma of the palace gradually fades as you trek deeper in. It takes you a while to notice that the flowers around you are withering the closer you get to the center of the palace. You smile, itching to eliminate whatever you find as you go deeper.

Push through.
