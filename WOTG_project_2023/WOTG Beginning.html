In the Beginning

This is you, a Victorian-era adventurer investigating a strange portal to another dimension. Your discoveries have led you to the basement of an abandoned pub in London. After shooting your way through a small horde of manic goblins, you came across the portal they were guarding with their lives. Your leader and monarch, Queen Victoria, insists that this mysterious portal be investigated thoroughly and destroyed. In this dark, dank cellar, standing in a pile of goblin guts, comes your first choice.

Will you enter the portal?

1) Honor Queen Victoria's wishes and enter the portal.
2) Disobey and hope the consequences don’t come back to haunt you.
