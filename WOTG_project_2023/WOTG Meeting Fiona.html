Meeting the Warriors

You extend your full arm through the portal and feel a cool breeze on your skin from the other side. Honoring the Queen, you decide to step into lands unknown. Immediately upon entering through the portal, you feel a painful strike to your head and are knocked to the ground by a blunt object. As you get back on your feet, you find a group of four warriors surrounding the portal who, judging by their faces, were expecting goblins and not a middle-aged man.

“What? Where are the goblins, Desmond?” Asks the obviously dimwitted young woman in the red jacket. She is trying to peer past you through the portal.

“Well Fiona, judging by the smell wafting through the portal, it seems our goblin problem has been taken care of courtesy of our friend here.” Replies the small rabbit in a thick Russian accent.

“OH. MY. GOD. I CAN’T believe I’m going to have to shower again now, this stink is NOT coming off.” Says the tall woman in purple, trying to air the stench out of her blouse.

The large insect creature remains silent.

“...Wait.” The dumb woman in front squints her eyes at you and finally acknowledges your existence. She sniffs in your general direction, then points her polearm at you. “You smell just like them! I can smell the goblin on you from a mile away. Draw your weapon and face our wrath, mongrel!”

The other warriors follow suit, readying themselves for a fight on her command. This could get messy.

How do you deal with the warriors?

1) Convince them you are not a foe, but an ally.
2) Attack them before they overpower you.
