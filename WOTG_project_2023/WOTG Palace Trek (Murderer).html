Trek to the Palace

You make your way up the mountain toward the palace that looms at the peak. Despite your adventerous nature, you have grown accustomed to the flat terrain of London and are profoundly exhausted when you get up the hill. Faced with two grand doors leading inside, there is only one thing to do. You swing open the doors and enter the palace.

Inside the palace, the first thing you are greeted with is a painting of a crying woman, clearly afflicted with some sort of illness. You cannot identify why she is sick and you do not care.

Ignore and walk past.
