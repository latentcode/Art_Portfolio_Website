Facing the Evil

You and your unlikely companion walk into the center of the room, making yourselves known to the figures before you. It’s the two of you against two adversaries, and you have two more bullets left in your revolver. The fight seems as balanced as it can be.

“Coward!” The tiefling in royal attire shouts at the woman next to you. “Do you know how high of an honor it is to have one of Them take an interest in you. Your body is the womb for something greater, something unknowable, Talax. You have the power to make the intangible tangible, to give birth to one of the most powerful Eldritch beings in the universe, right here in Athas. And yet you run?!”

“I won’t end up like her. I can’t.” Talax murmurs, distraught. You realize who she’s talking about. The woman from the painting isn’t sick, she’s pregnant. Inside her lies an Eldritch creature slowly developing from within, with limbs so long it had to burst through her head to make room. Soon, it will crawl out of her and seize control of this world and yours. A similar fate will happen to Talax if she does not close the portals.

With a deep breath, Talax raises her glowing staff as high as she can and the portal on the floor starts to close. Horrifyingly, the creature inside of the disfigured woman begins to get pulled out of her. Its arm fully extends through the head of the woman, followed by a tangled mess of other fleshy, gangly limbs being ripped out through her body. She stumbles to the floor as the creature is violently sucked toward the ever shrinking portal.

In using the staff, the Eldritch entity inside of Talax is being viscerally ripped out too. The eyes on her body begin to bulge and pop and she falls to her knees. Despite everything, she keeps her staff raised high, determined to close the portal no matter what.

Eyes wide, the tiefling lunges at Talax. You try to stop her, but she blasts you away with a surge of force, shooting you fifteen feet back. Pushing Talax to the ground, the tiefling grabs the staff and throws it toward the portal before beginning to conjure a devestating spell. The furious tiefling only wants one thing: to kill the traitor beneath her.

You only have a moment to act before it’s too late.

What choice do you make?

1) Pick up the staff.
2) Protect Talax.
