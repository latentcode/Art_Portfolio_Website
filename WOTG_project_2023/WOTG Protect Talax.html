Protecting Talax

Without a second thought, you sprint over to Talax and tackle the tiefling on top of her. The two of you tumble across the room, but you manage to keep the tiefling’s arms and legs restrained. Unshaken by your efforts, she rams her horns into your head, dazing you and weakening your grip. She uses this opportunity to worm out of your grasp and get to her feet.

You reach for your revolver, taking a bead on her head when she kicks your gun across the room. Standing above you, she smashes her shoe into your face with the fury of a thousand Gods. As she begins to do so again, you grab her leg and pull her back down to the floor. You scramble to get behind her, trying to put her in a chokehold, when you notice something. Fifteen feet away, you watch as Talax’s staff falls into the portal. In your hesitation, the tiefling elbows you twice in the head and breaks your nose.

Seething with rage, the tiefling wraps her glowing hands around your neck and begins searing your skin. You jab her in the side, but that only seems to make the pain burn hotter. The sensation is utterly excruciating. You try pulling her hands away, but it doesn’t take long for all the strength to leave your body. Your eyes start to close, your breathing falters. This is the end. You have no choice but to relent to the darkness.

Just as everything around you begins to fade, you hear two shots ring out from behind you. Then, you feel the pressure on your neck disappear. You manage to open your eyes. In front of you, the tiefling places her hands over two bloodied wounds in her torso. Your heart rate spikes. You feel each beat pound against your chest. Your mind goes blank, save for one unrelenting thought: Close the portal, save the world.

Your arms tense up with anticipation. A wave of strength washes over you as you shove the tiefling to the floor. With considerable effort, you get to your feet and look down at the injured woman below you. She still has a whole world of fight left in her. The only way to stop this chaos is by getting rid of her as soon as possible.

Beside you, a wounded Talax stumbles in, wielding your smoking revolver in her hand. You turn and look into her eyes. Battered and beaten, the two of you nod at each other, knowing full well what has to be done. Taking both her arms, you and Talax drag the tiefling toward the portal. As she nears the center of the room, she starts to radiate with lightning, channeling some unnatural force within her.

“I won’t let you kill me!” The tiefling shrieks as her eyes go white.

Itching to prove her wrong, you and Talax bring her to the edge. In one fell swoop, the two of you kick her into the portal behind her, launching her into the unknowable chasm she fought so hard to protect.

As she falls, you watch her explode with electrical energy, shooting beams of lightning everywhere as she disappears from sight. With the two summoners dead, the portal finally closes.

Take in the quiet of the room.
