Talk It Out

You laugh nervously and shrug at the tiefling. "Look ma’am, obviously I wasn’t going to kill you. I just wanted to test your guys’ reflexes. You’re really something else lady, I mean, my goodness. That other girl was WILD too haha." There's a deafening silence. You cough awkwardly, "Now, how about you guys make your dynamic duo a dynamic trio, what do you say?"

“Stop talking,” the tiefling replies, clearly annoyed.

In the corner of your eye, you see the arm barreling toward you. You gulp and smile nervously right before it smashes you into the ground and then pulverizes whatever pieces are left. It seems your rizz deficiency was your downfall.
