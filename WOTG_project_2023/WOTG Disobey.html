Disobeying the Queen

You’ve entered one too many mysterious glowing portals in your life. It’s time to put your foot down, enough is enough. Honestly, it’s surprising you hadn’t stopped portal hopping earlier, especially considering the horrendous trauma you endured during your last venture into the unknown. But now the important question: what do you say to the Queen?

In your nervousness you begin to zone out, running through a handful of possible excuses in your mind. Then, you think of your punishment if she ever found out you’ve been shirking your duties. You reflect on the beheadings and hangings that still plague your homeland. You think of your wife’s father who met a brutal and terrible fate at the hand of Queen Victoria. In your absentmindedness, you trip over a severed goblin arm and fall head-first into one of the steps of the cellar stairs.

A loud cartoon sound effect echoes through the basement as you go limp.

Your soul floats away. You have never had an out of body experience before, but you thank God that your soul is floating up and not down. However, it is not God that awaits you. Awaiting you is something even more powerful, something even more cruel: the divine Yellow M&M™.

For what feels like days, he stares at you. Then, without warning, his eye twitches and he raises his left hand decisively. Bad news pal, it’s thumbs down.

Your soul is flung downward at an unimaginable speed, and the last thing you see is the Yellow M&M’s™ eyes as your vision turns black.
