The Aftermath

A moment of silence passes. The four of us watch the portal get smaller and smaller, unsure of what to say or how to feel. Finally, with the portal almost closed, the rabbit speaks up, “She could still be alive down there. Enna, my sweet Enna. I have to save her.” He takes a step forward and begins to fall.

Suddenly, a hand grabs him by his robe. “No, I won’t let you,” says Fiona, holding the rabbit up over the abyss. She has tears in her eyes. It takes everything she has not to start sobbing. “You’ll die down there. And we need you, Desmond.”

The grim expression on his face gives way to sadness, then acceptance. “...You’re right,” he says hoarsely. With a sigh of relief, Fiona pulls him up to solid ground and we watch as the portal finally closes. Whatever was left of the mound of flesh slinks back into nothingness.

You and the others tend to your wounds, ripping fabric off your clothes to use as bandages. The rabbit examines Fiona’s injuries. The injury to her collarbone is severe and her left arm needs medical attention, but she will live to fight another day.

You are utterly exhausted. But nevertheless, a thought pops into your head that makes your heart drop. “I have to get back. I have to get back to London before the entry portal closes!”

The other three look at you. The rabbit peers into your eyes and nods in understanding. A sincere smile forms on his face, “Then we’ve got to get going.”

Fiona beams. Wiping the tears from her eyes, she says, “Yeah goblin man, we’re with you all the way!”

“Manny, let’s go,” The rabbit says to the insectoid. With a grunt of affirmation, the large creature scoops the three of you in his arms and jogs out the room. On the way, Fiona takes out a crayon box with the words, “To Fiona, From Enna” written on it fancifully. She holds it close to her chest and smiles somberly.

With the portal closing fast on the horizon, Manny sprints as fast as he can toward it. The rabbit looks over at you, looking quite cozy in Manny’s arms, and nods respectfully, “Thank you, friend, for everything. Manny, Fiona, and I must head back into town and continue training. May we fight everyday with your bravery.” He pounds his chest in appreciation.

Fiona sniffles and looks up at you too, with that big dumb smile she always sports, “You’re not such a bad guy after all, goblin man.” You smile back, just about to say something when Manny picks you up.

Without hesitation, he chucks you through the small opening in the portal, making you spiral like a football. You can immediately tell when you’ve crossed into London, with the smell of the city only overpowered by the smell of rotten goblin guts before you.

In your heart of hearts, you feel that you’ll be seeing them again, somewhere along your crazy journey. But for now, you need to tell Queen Victoria about your victory. You are the savior of London, England after all.

ENDING (5/9): Savior of the Commonwealth
