Closing the Portal

You race toward the staff, teetering on the edge of the portal, and grab it before it falls in. You raise it as high as you possibly can. It's up to you to end this madness, once and for all.

You watch the rotten mass of flesh slink back into the void as the portal rapidly closes. Suddenly, you hear Talax scream in pain behind you. Turning your head, you lock eyes with the abomination emerging from her stomach. The massive creature unfurls from inside her, as grotesque as the creature you saw earlier. Knocking the tiefling across the room, the creature screams as it flies toward the portal. The only thing that stands in its way is you. Moving quickly, you try to dodge the vile aberration, but it moves so unpredictably that you have no safe place to run.

At the last second it collides with you. You scream out in anguish as the monster drags you down into the abyss with it. Falling into nothingness, you look up at the portal above you, still just barely open. You search for your staff, but it's nowhere to be seen; without that staff, the portal will never be fully sealed. This is the last thought you're able to process before you lose conciousness, falling at impossible speeds toward the ground.

Upon waking up, you find yourself in a realm so incomprehensible that there is nothing from your own world to compare it to. Looking up, you can see planets being ravaged by an Eldritch plague. A tear falls from your eye as you think of your wife somewhere out there. From thin air, one of Them appears in front of you, millions of stories tall with the appearance of the very people it is conquering. You fire your last two shots at it, desperate to end this nightmare, but the bullets do not fire. Perhaps they never existed in the first place.

It reaches down toward you and embraces you in a final hug.

“We Have Risen. Welcome to Our New World.”
