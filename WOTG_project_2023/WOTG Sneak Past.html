Nuh uh

Your mind only registers one word: “nope”. Whatever this lady is up to is none of your business. She can rummage through as many cardboard boxes as the world throws at her, it has nothing to do with you. Honestly, good for her. Who says a girl can’t have hobbies?

You creep past the stack of wooden crates you were using as cover. She doesn’t react as you sneak toward the exit of the valley, but you can feel the eyes on her body watching you. She may not have noticed your presence, but something else definitely has.

Once you leave that claustrophobic pathway, you immediately notice a mountain about a mile in front of you. On top lies a dark palace, practically begging you to investigate it. That is where you need to head, you are sure of it. Nothing that ominous isn’t important.

Continue forth by yourself.
