Seize the Power

Somewhat hesitantly, you walk to the center of the room and announce your intentions to the two women: “Hello! Would you mind if I partake in some of that… otherworldly power?” You raise your eyebrows up and down, which is extremely convincing and not weird at all.

In the corner of your eye you see the tiefling in royal attire conjure up a spell, summoning a great wall of thorns in front of the doorway, fully blocking the only entrance and exit. If you had anybody with you, they surely won't be able to help you now.

The disfigured woman takes this opportunity to sprint directly at you and tackle you before you can react. “HEY! Get off me!” you shout.

The tiefling looks toward you, then at her companion, “Lyanna, confiscate his weapons.” The woman restraining you bares her teeth in a wide, malicious grin, then takes your Colt revolver. That was your Plan B, so you better hope your Plan A works.

Once the two women deem you harmless enough, they let you go and walk back to their positions in front of the portal. You ask your first question, “So what exactly is that thing climbing out of the portal? It looks revolting, how is this thing going to bestow us with power?”

“No one said it would,” the tiefling replies emotionless, “You made that assumption.” The hand coming through the other woman’s head twitches slightly, then extends further from the skull and weakly gestures at the tiefling. Acknowledging it, she continues, “But power it does posses. Nothing in all the realms has seen anything like this. Unfathomable power… Eldritch power. This ‘revolting’ entity crawling through the portal is the hivemind, the only existence of an Eldritch God here in our realms. But it is immaterial, unable to interact with our world, utterly useless without its followers. That's where my sister comes in.”

The tiefling extends her arms outward, “We are the harbingers of Their New World. Through them we will know our place in the unknowable. In acceptance there is power. You will know this power too, sooner or later.”

“There were so many red flags in everything you just said.” You reply, laughing nervously. You think it's a good idea to start crawling away from the entity now.

One of the thorns from the wall extends toward you rapidly and impales your shoulder. You are lifted toward the women, hovering just above the portal.

“Witness our faces. Witness us, for we are the last thing you will ever be able to comprehend."

You are dropped through the portal. With the last of your brainpower, you come to terms with failing your Queen. You are a part of something greater now. You have found your place amongst Them.
