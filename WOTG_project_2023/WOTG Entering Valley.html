Into the Valley

You listen to your instincts. You aren't sure what these people are capable of and you certainly don't want to find out. You know better than to trust the first people you see in a foreign world, especially when they outnumber you. In one fluid motion, you draw your revolver and fan the hammer, firing four bullets within the span of a second. A tumbleweed tumbles past. Somewhere in the vast desolate wasteland before you, you hear a distant clock chime twelve noon. The warriors have been dealt with.

A shiver shoots up your spine once they fall, as if by eliminating these four people you have satisfied some dark urge deep inside. Best not to dwell on it, it's probably nothing.

Without a destination in mind, you begin wandering, something you’ve grown used to doing in your line of work. You can’t make heads or tails of this place as you venture forth; it’s unlike anything you’ve ever seen. The distant clock, even more distant now, chimes six as you come across a narrow pathway surrounded by mountains on either side. There is only one way through to the rest of the region. One way in, one way out.

You walk through confidently until you hear the clattering of metallic objects being thrown. Rummaging through cardboard boxes, you spot a ghoulish woman donning a wizard’s outfit. Littered across her body are eyeballs of varying sizes, blinking at different rates. She hasn't noticed you yet.

What will you do with the stranger?

1) Introduce yourself!
2) Sneak past.
3) Attack her before she can react.
