Interruption

You shoot at the magic tiefling, knocking her off-balance and causing her to miss her attacks. The elf rushes over and knees her in the stomach, making the tiefling fall to the floor.

While the rabbit and the insectoid run closer, you watch as the disfigured woman brings her cleaver down on Fiona. She manages to squirm out of the way so that the weapon misses her head, but it lands in her clavicle instead, causing irreparable damage to her body. If that wasn’t enough, the arm protruding out of the woman’s head extends to full length, cracking and popping as it does so. The arm, now ten feet long, effortlessly grabs your companion, the tall elf, and throws her into the portal, erasing any trace of her in this realm. The rabbit yells out, heartbroken.

From afar you notice the insectoid jump on the Eldritch arm, trying to wrangle it into submission. You turn your attention to the disfigured woman and chuck your empty Colt revolver at her before she can land another strike. Quickly, you run over and push the woman off Fiona. She drops her cleaver on the ground and looks utterly drained, as if she has only just begun processing her injuries. Using all of your strength, you pick up the disfigured woman by her collar and drag her to the edge of the portal. With one strong kick, you shove her into the unknown, never to be seen again.

The Eldritch arm attached to her gets dragged down with her. Just before it slips into the portal, the insectoid successfully jumps off and lands on his back. Looking up from the portal, you see the little rabbit standing over the unconscious body of the tiefling, his eyes wide and his face grim. He drags the tiefling over and dumps her into the portal as well. Not long after, the portal begins closing…

Take a deep breath.
