The Aftermath

You hurry over to where the group has gathered, kneeling over a figure on the floor.

“My beautiful girls,” the rabbit quietly utters, lying on his back, “And my beloved Manny, our most loyal, most precious companion.” A tear rolls down the rabbit's furry cheek. “Listen closely, you three. Follow the stranger out of here, he can accompany you on your journey back home. I entrust him with your safety.” He coughs violently for a moment.

“Wait a second!” You interject, “I’m really sorry, but I have to get home too. The portal out of here is closing!”

“We can take care of ourselves, Des,” the elf says sadly, “We’ll be fine, really.”

“I know Enna, but I won’t be able to rest easy until I can make sure. Please, for me.” The rabbit turns to look at you, with a serious expression on his face. “I know I am asking a lot, way too much from someone who just met us. But you looked out for us here. You protected my precious Fiona. Please, look after them all a little longer. I promise you, when the time is right, you’ll have your chance to go back home.”

You think of your wife, waiting day and night for you to come back home. You think of Queen Victoria furious at your absence. But some things have to be done. “Okay.” You sigh, “Dammit rabbit, I’ll do it.”

“I’m not a goddamn rabbit,” He chuckles, “But thank you anyway, friend. It truly was an honor…” The rabbit’s eyes get heavier, “Enna, Fiona, Manny. Maybe try to put my name on a plaque somewhere when you get the chance,” he laughs. With his last breath, he utters something in Russian, then fades away.

After a while, the four of you exit the palace and make your way on foot toward their village. It is a long walk into town, littered with violent wildlife and rough terrain. You fight your way through mutant bears and giant snakes, doing your damndest to keep your friends safe. But, more often than not, it's them guarding you.

You finally make it to the edge of town after a three-day journey. “Will you guys be alright from here?” you ask, entering the front gates.

“Once I finally get this stench off me, I’ll be fine,” Enna replies. She sighs, “Thank you though, for easing Des’ mind. I know it wasn’t an easy choice to make. All that man did was try to protect us. Now it's our turn to do the protecting.” She smiles, then heads off down the street.

“Thanks a lot, friend.” Fiona says sincerely. “I hope you can get back to your wife soon. But since you're staying here a while, our monastery is always open. Or the tavern. Ooo! Manny, do you wanna go to the tavern?”

Manny nods enthusiastically.

“I’ll see you later, goblin man. You're welcome to join us anytime!” She beams back at you before heading down the road. Manny gives a salute before he follows along.

The sun sets on the homely town before you. You don't know how long you have to stay here, but you know what? It doesn't seem all that bad. You can definitely get used to the peace and quiet.

ENDING (6/9): Home away from Home
