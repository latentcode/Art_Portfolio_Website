Your Pound of Flesh

You knew how this was going to end once you fired your fifth shot and dropped your fifth body. Violence is the only thing you truly understand. Although you’ve tried to avoid it before, you know it is the only reliable way of getting results. This time is no different.

Standing your ground in front of the doorway, you stomp your right foot into the floor as hard as possible and cock back the hammer of your revolver. The two women turn to face you, with a look on their faces as if they know what you’re capable of. A smile creeps up the disfigured woman’s face. She has known bloodlust too.

Almost immediately, two rays of fire fly toward you. The tiefling in royal clothes stays behind, focusing on knocking you off balance with her spells. You barely manage to dodge them both as the smiling woman charges you with a butcher’s cleaver. She catches you in the side, inflicting a burning wound on your hip. You manage to shove the woman back when you feel another beam of fire cut through your cheek. The tiefling begins running toward you, attempting to join her friend in overwhelming you.

Off balance, you stumble backward into the doorframe. Without missing a beat, the smiling woman throws her cleaver directly into your left shoulder and begins to lunge. The pain is excruciating. Her only mistake was hitting the wrong shoulder. With the two monsters aligned, you raise your shooting arm and fire your last bullet through both of their chests. You chuckle as you watch their upper bodies explode in a mist of blood. Your gun shouldn't have been strong enough to take them out, but there was something special about this bullet. It was infused by something blasphemous- a sinister essence that tore through anything in its way. Their flesh didn't stand a chance.

As their bodies collapse, yours follows suit. Not because of the pain, but because of the evil that has awakened in you. Your mind fills with images of a woman, so very familiar looking. She whispers to you, ever so softly,

"Embrace my bloodlust."
