A want, not a need

You reach around for something to write with, but find nothing. The strange eyeball woman has already breezed past you down the corridor and now you have to jog to catch up. What a horrible turn of events this is, being refused the right to express yourself creatively. You are fairly certain this is the worst thing that will happen to you on this journey. The burden on your soul grows a little heavier.

Jog further into the palace.
