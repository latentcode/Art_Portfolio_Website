Interruption

You shoot the disfigured woman directly in her head and she pauses for a moment, dazed. Fiona uses the last of her strength to drag the woman off of her with her good arm and onto the floor. She then sits up and presses her knee on her enemy’s back to restrain her. You approach the woman from her head and attempt to pull out the Eldritch arm with all your might. You feel the flesh stretching and tearing, before you hear a loud snap. The arm pops out of its socket and you are able to rip it from her head. Though it tries to wriggle free from your grasp, you now wield an Eldritch arm.

Across the room, the tiefling manages to shoot her beams of fire, striking the insectoid in his shoulder armor, the elf in her gut, and the rabbit directly in his heart. As you look up from the near-dead woman beneath you, you see the insectoid charge across the room and ram the tiefling with his shoulder, knocking her across the room. You know what you have to do.

As the elf rushes over to tend to the rabbit, the rest of us approach the tiefling…

Grip the arm even tighter.
