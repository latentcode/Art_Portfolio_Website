Befriending the Warriors

Thinking fast on your feet, you clarify, “No this is just goblin viscera. From all the goblins I killed. Like the rabbit said, I am no friend of goblins. I ventured here on behalf of Q-”

You feel a rock hit your head. “I am no rabbit.” Says the rabbit, arming himself with more rocks.

“WOAH my bad!” You exclaim as you process your minor concussion. “All I want to do is close this portal and stop all this madness.” You rub the bump on your head sadly.

The tall lady in purple looks at her companions, having given up on ridding herself of her foul odor. “As RUDE as this guy is,” she remarks while rolling her eyes, “he does want the same thing as us.”

“True,” the dumb woman wisely mutters, “Five hands is better than four.”

The rabbit exclaims an expletive in Russian, “You mean ten hands! Have you forgotten our math lessons, Fiona?”

The two women and the rabbit begin walking away, bickering about basic mathematics. The insectoid pauses, turns back to you, and nods reassuringly. He ushers for you to follow. You can’t help feeling at ease with your newfound compatriots. The wasteland you have found yourself in feels a little less lonely.

Continue forth with your allies.
