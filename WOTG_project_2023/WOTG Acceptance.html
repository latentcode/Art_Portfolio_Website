Acceptance

You curse yourself for not bringing more bullets. You sealed your fate when you wasted thirty shots taking out that goblin horde at the beginning. But none of that matters now. You drop your empty revolver and put your hands out to the side, gesturing that you’re not going to fight back anymore.

The arm slowly recovers from its devastating impact with the wall. It cracks its bones back into place and prepares for another lunge. You close your eyes and await what’s coming.

To your surprise, nothing happens. You wiggle your fingers and pat your torso; you’re still alive. Opening your eyes, you see the pile of flesh in the portal pulsing slower now. You look back at the arm, and watch it push and twist itself back into the woman’s skull. She stands up and limps toward you, barely managing to keep herself from collapsing.

“You do not shy away from your death?” The tiefling asks, puzzled, still standing next to the portal.

“I don’t shy away from anything,” you lie. Damn, you feel real cool right now.

The tiefling raises her eyebrow then looks at the person to your right. To your amazement, the horribly wounded woman made it to you, bleeding and broken, but smiling. Though you did not think it was possible, she speaks. It is only a few words, spoken in an otherworldly, guttural voice:

“You're perfect.”

Before you can fully process this, she grabs the back of your head and shoves a bunch of the arm’s discarded flesh into your mouth. The sensation is indescribable, but you know it is the most disgusting thing that’s ever happened to you.

“It’s time for you to become like us.”

You resist and push her away but not before you swallow it all. You shudder and fall to your knees, trying to cough up what you just consumed.

“Another child of the New World,” the tiefling remarks coldly before turning her attention back to the portal.

Something unnatural stirs within your stomach. You begin to feel sweaty and weak. It’s not long before you lose your desire to remove what’s inside of you. You feel an overwhelming sense of fatherly love toward it. You don’t even feel yourself walking next to the two women and taking your place beside them. You don’t feel your skin begin to slough off or your head begin to expand. All you feel is a connection, one that you’ve been longing for all your life.

___

Two hundred years later, a group of humans venture out in the middle of the desert looking for their missing friend, completely unaware of the Eldritch plague consuming their planet. One of them notices a slimy trail in the sand and tells her friends to follow along. They come across you.

You’ve been doing your best to look like them. You feel you didn’t get their faces exactly right, but it has been a while since you’ve seen a human. You wonder if they would still appreciate your attempt.

The voice in the back of your mind echoes through your body. It tells you to consume them. These ones are still untouched by Our plague. They have yet to know the beauty of Our New World. They need you to show them what it means to be a God.

You happily oblige.
