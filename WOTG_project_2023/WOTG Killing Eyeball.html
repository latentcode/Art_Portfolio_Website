Instinct

Smoke rises from your barrel. You fired a round through the biggest eye you could see - a bullseye that was begging to be struck. As the monster falls to the floor, its head snaps backward so it can look you in the eyes. Expressionless, it opens its mouth and begins to scream. Dozens of voices ring out until everything goes silent and the monster finally slumps over.

You’ve felt this feeling before. It’s been decades, but you’ve felt it, embraced it, relished it. Bloodlust. The dark urge you felt before has only come back stronger. This path you’re on only has one end. You’ve avoided it before, but not this time. You will close that portal no matter who gets in the way.

Upon exiting the valley, you spot a dark palace atop a mountain. It is there you will find your answers. It is there you will fulfill your destiny.

Seek your pound of flesh.
