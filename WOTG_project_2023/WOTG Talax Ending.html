The Aftermath

You and Talax take a deep breath in. Against all odds, you managed to stave off an Eldritch takeover and save the realms from Their unthinkable influence. Not a bad thing to put on your CV, you think. Next to you, your companion falls to her knees, sobbing quietly.

“I can’t believe we did it,” she whispers through the tears. “Never in a million years did I think I’d be rid of Them.” She seems more whole now, like a piece of her personality has returned to her.

“Are you sure it’s over? What about the thing inside of you?” You ask.

“It’s quiet now. It’s not kicking or screaming or clawing. Without a brain, it will die out. And then I’ll be free.” You notice the eyeballs on her body begin to shrink. She smiles and turns to you. “Come here.”

You kneel down beside her, as thankful as she is that it’s over. The woman looks you in the eyes and reaches out her hand. Before you know it, she takes your broken nose and sets it back into place. The pain surges violently, then calms down.

“OW, lady!” You wince.

“Sorry,” she chuckles, “I always find it easier to take when there’s no warning.”

“How did you end up in this situation anyway?” You ask, rubbing your nose.

“Curiosity. Power. Recognition. You name it. They promised me an irresistible opportunity, a chance to cement my name in history. Truth be told, the consequences didn’t cross my mind until it was too late.” She wipes her tears. “But that’s all over with.” She stands up and extends her arm, helping you to your feet.

“Where will you go now?” You ask.

She sighs, “Y’know, I was banking on the whole 'Eldritch plague' thing being my claim to fame. But getting to kick those people’s ass was pretty damn satisfying too. I’ve always traveled alone, but I never knew how fun it was to stir things up with someone else. If you’d have me, I think we’d make a pretty solid team.”

“You know something, I think you might be right about that.” You say hopefully. Then you remember something important. “But the portal back to London is closing! If we’re gonna stick together, we have to make it there before we get shut out forever.”

She furrows her eyebrows and grins, “Follow me.”

You and Talax race to the back of the palace where you reach a gaunt horse tied up to a pillar. Talax strokes his mane as you quickly untie the ropes. Once the two of you hop on his back, Talax clicks her tongue and the horse races off toward the portal. Though it is nearly closed when you get there, the horse manages to leap through what tiny gap still remained and land inside the all-to-familiar environment of London, England. You are home at last.

"Woah, is this your handiwork?" Talax asks, staring down at the pile of goblin guts below you.

"Yes ma'am," you say proudly, pinching your nose to protect it from the horrid smell.

"Hell yeah, dude!" She says, extending her hand. You high five each other in style, before guiding your horse out of the cellar into the city proper.

It’s been years since you’ve had a partner, but something about this woman is special. You can tell the two of you are going to make one hell of a duo.
