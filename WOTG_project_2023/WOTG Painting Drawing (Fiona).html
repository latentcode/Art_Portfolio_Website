Perfection

You reach around for something to write with, but find nothing. Noticing what you are trying to do, Fiona gasps excitedly. She searches through her satchel and pulls out a half-used crayon.

“Here you go, goblin man!” She smiles. It is clear she is no stranger to drawing mustaches on serious paintings.

You seize the day and draw the twirliest mustache you can. Fiona joins in with another few colors and you both make what can only be described as a modern-day Mona Lisa. You feel a catharsis deep in your soul. This was totally worth it. Carpe diem.

Saunter further into the palace.
