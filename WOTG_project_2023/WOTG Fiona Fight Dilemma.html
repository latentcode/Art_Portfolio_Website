The Battle

In one fluid motion, you step forward, aim down the sights of your gun, and fire two shots at the woman with the hand through her head. The bullets hit her shoulder and chest, but she takes them in stride, not even flinching at the impact. That being said, your presence did catch them both entirely off guard. At your sides, you feel your friends run past you into the fray, seizing their moment to strike. Thanks to you, they were able to encircle the two women before they could react.

The insectoid rushes the tiefling in the fancy attire, lunging at the woman. She dodges at the last minute, narrowly avoiding his tackle, but landing right next to the small rabbit. As if it were a dance, the rabbit twirls around and strikes the woman twice on her head. Then, he takes his mighty branch weapon and delivers a powerful blow to her gut. The tiefling staggers backwards. In retaliation, she ignites the floor beneath them in fire, blasting them both away from her. They attempt to recover, singed and injured, while she runs to the aid of her companion.

The woman with the hand through her head stands her ground against Fiona and the elf. Your two companions got in a few solid strikes while the disfigured woman was surprised, but now she is ready to kill. With her cleaver, she makes three enormous swipes at Fiona, tearing through her left arm.

You hurry in, firing three shots at the woman with the cleaver. The bullets wound her severely, but don't manage to break her aggression. She knocks Fiona to the floor and raises her cleaver above Fiona’s head.

At the same time, the tiefling readies three devastating beams of fire in her hands, preparing to hurl them at the rabbit, the insectoid, and the elf. You have only a single shot left to interrupt one of the enemies.

Who will you shoot?

1) Shoot the spellcasting tiefling.
2) Shoot the woman with the cleaver.
